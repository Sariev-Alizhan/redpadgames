## 8. Visual style rules (so the agent renders consistently)

1. **Always dark**. Background is always `#0A0A0B` or pure black. Never light mode.
2. **Type only in white or accent red**. Never grey, never gradient on text.
3. **One accent word per headline** in red. Everything else white.
4. **No drop shadows on type**. Crisp Inter Black, kerned tight.
5. **Logo placement**: bottom-left or top-left, transparent PNG, never inside a white box.
6. **Square (1:1) for feed**. Stories are 9:16 — re-crop key art respecting the character / focal point.
7. **Quiet motion**. If the agent generates Reels, slow Ken-Burns drift over key art beats hyperactive cuts. Tempo: ~6 seconds per beat.
8. **Music**: Reels can use Seven Nation Army (the studio is currently using it for the live site). Otherwise instrumental industrial / cinematic-bass.
9. **Don't invent metrics**. Numbers in this brief are verified. If a number isn't here, don't post it.
10. **Don't use the word "AAA" as a self-description**. Use "premium multiplayer" or just specifics ("70 specialists", "64 km² wasteland").

## 9. What NOT to claim

These are common AI-fill mistakes — please avoid:

- ❌ "Founded by" anyone other than Vildan Fazylov + Yevgeniy Neverov
- ❌ Claiming Dustland is on PlayStation/Xbox/iOS/Android **today** — those
  platforms are "planned", only Steam is live
- ❌ Quoting D1/D3 retention numbers publicly. The internal closed-test
  metrics (13,600 / 65% / 34% / $8.30 CPA) are NOT externally verifiable —
  use the **40,000+ stress test** number instead
- ❌ Implying the Tencent partnership is publishing / investment / IPO. It
  is a cloud-services agreement
- ❌ Generating Vildan or Yevgeniy quotes that aren't in this brief
- ❌ Using Dustland Delivery (an unrelated game by Wildmax) imagery or copy
